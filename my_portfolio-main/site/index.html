// About me

I started with curiosity. I stayed for the craft.

01 · The foundation

It started in Comilla, with school, science and a lot of hard work.

I finished both SSC and HSC in science with a perfect 5.00 GPA. Somewhere along the way, computers stopped being just a tool and became the thing I wanted to understand.

02 · BRAC CSE

In 2022 I started Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University. I joined the Computer Club and the Robotics Club, went to workshops and tech events, and learned that I like building things more than just reading about them.

03 · Building for the web

My first real projects were PHP and MySQL web apps: a book community, an inventory dashboard and a barbershop booking site. Each one taught me something new about databases, users and finishing what I start.

04 · Going deeper

Then came research. For my thesis, our team built a crowd density estimation model with CSRNet++ and attention. I also explored generative music models and built a 3D game in OpenGL. In 2025, our AI food-safety idea reached the Next Gen Ideathon finals.

05 · Real work

Internships at ZENTORRA and now Infusions Tech showed me how real teams work: clear tasks, good documentation and steady progress. I graduated in 2026, and I'm ready for my first full-time developer role.

I'm still learning.
